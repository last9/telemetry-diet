# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community where people can participate regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Give and receive technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Credit other people's work and protect confidential information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory, insulting, or demeaning language or imagery.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal and political attacks.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or telemetry without permission.
- Impersonation, deliberate misinformation, or evasion of moderation.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the Telemetry Diet community.

## Reporting

Report a possible violation privately to [hello@last9.io](mailto:hello@last9.io) with the subject `Telemetry Diet conduct report`. Include relevant links or screenshots and describe any immediate safety concern. Do not post sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible, review reports in good faith, and give involved parties a fair opportunity to provide context. Retaliation against someone for raising a concern is itself a violation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, limit participation temporarily, or ban a participant permanently. The response will be proportionate to the impact, severity, and pattern of behavior. Serious safety threats may skip intermediate steps.

Questions about this Code may be sent to the same contact address.
